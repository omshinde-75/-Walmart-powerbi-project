## 📌 Introduction
Welcome to the **Walmart Market Business Intelligence Dashboard** project! This repository contains an end-to-end data analytics solution built using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides dynamic and insight-driven business intelligence for Walmart Market, a multinational retail giant, analyzing operations across **Canada, Mexico, and the United States**.

By transforming complex transactional data into interactive visual stories, this project empowers executive decision-makers to quickly gauge regional performance, product brand profitability, and overall revenue trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
The primary goal of this project is to simulate real-time market reporting by addressing the following objectives:
1. **Performance Tracking:** Monitor and compare Total Transactions, Total Profit, Total Returns, and Revenue against Target goals.
2. **Geographical Drill-Down:** Understand regional performance down to the store city level across three North American countries.
3. **Brand Analysis:** Identify high-performing product brands, analyze profit margins, and monitor product return trends.
4. **Benchmarking:** Utilize previous month (PM) metrics as benchmarks to evaluate current month (CM) results.

## 💡 Key Features Implemented

### 🔹 Advanced Visualizations
- **Matrix Visuals:** Displays Total Transactions, Profit, Profit Margin, and Return Rate by Product Brand. Integrated with conditional formatting (Data Bars and Color Scales) for quick visual cues.
- **KPI Cards:** Dynamic scorecards tracking Current Month vs. Last Month for Transactions, Profit, and Returns. Implemented "Low is Good" conditional formatting for Return rates.
- **Interactive Map & Treemap:** Maps visualize transactions by store city, filterable by country. The Treemap enables a seamless drill-down experience: *Country → State → City*.
- **Gauge Charts:** Visualizes current month revenue against the previous month’s target to provide a quick executive summary of goal attainment.
- **Trend Columns:** Weekly revenue tracking for precise time-series performance monitoring.

### 🔹 User Experience (UX) & Storytelling
- **Bookmarks & Navigation:** Created guided analytical paths, including a specific bookmark for identifying major milestones (e.g., *📍 Portland hits 1,000 sales in December*).
- **Custom Tooltips & Interactions:** Refined cross-filtering behaviors between visuals to ensure clean and relevant data interactions.

## 📈 Top Business Insights Uncovered

✅ **Milestone Achieved:** The city of Portland reached a significant milestone of 1,000+ sales in December, marking it as a high-performing metropolitan area.  
🏆 **Brand Dominance:** The Top 10 Product Brands account for **25% of total revenue**, consistently exceeding their revenue targets.  
⚠️ **Return Rate Warning:** While Current Month Transactions and Profits showed solid growth, the overall Return Rate increased by **2.9%**. This signals a need for a quality assurance review on recent product lines.  
🌎 **Market Growth:** The **Mexico Market** significantly outperformed the previous month in both Profit and Revenue, indicating strong market reception and suggesting a need for increased resource allocation and investment in that region.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Core tool for Data Visualization, Dashboarding, and Reporting.
- **Data Modeling:** Star Schema design for optimized query performance.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Extensive use of DAX for calculating time intelligence, profit margins, conditional formatting logic, and KPIs.
- **Power Query:** Data extraction, cleaning, and transformation (ETL).

## 🚀 How to Use This Project

1. **Prerequisites:** Ensure you have the latest version of [Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop/) installed on your Windows machine.
2. **Download:** Clone or download this repository to your local machine.
3. **Open:** Double-click the `Wallmart Market Report(Power BI).pbix` file to open the dashboard.
4. **Explore:** Use the slicers on the left/top panels to filter by Country, Date, or Category. Click on map bubbles or matrix rows to see cross-highlighted data across the dashboard!
